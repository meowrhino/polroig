A tribute to the enigmatic musical action by Carles Santos and Pere Portabella during their imprisonment at La Model, of which no documentary record remains. A static procession of eight tubas, led by the violinist and composer, rises above six white pedestals arranged along the prison’s fourth gallery. Through sound and the resonance of the space, the eight musicians process towards an irretrievable past without moving from their positions.
