*Concierto barroco* is a musical and scenic exploration of the novel of the same title, written by Alejo Carpentier in 1974. In a dialogue-free staging, a series of cultural objects serve as a narrative thread, alongside the performance of different musical works drawn from the novel. The musicians themselves, portrayed as the composers and characters who appear in the novel, become the protagonists of an exercise that seeks to reconnect the musical works with their original contexts, dismantling the historical sources behind the fictional device that allows Carpentier to imagine the gaps in the official narrative.

Two panels bearing projected text hang above the stage and function as a supplement, re-signifying and exhaustively cataloguing—with Carpentier’s characteristic irony—everything displayed in this imaginary museum and brought within the viewer’s sight and hearing.
