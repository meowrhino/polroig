Catalan writer Mercè Rodoreda made this shirt for Nicole Fenosa during her exile in Paris, where she sewed as a means of survival. The shirt, preserved in the museum, serves as the starting point for an exhibition that explores exile and the relationship between Rodoreda and the Fenosa family.

A cotton thread hangs from the shirt and runs through the exhibition space, connecting the artworks. Along its path, small alterations, magnified through magnifying glasses, form a sequence of almost imperceptible events, translating into space the minimal gesture of constructing a narrative. Domestic gestures, invisible labour, and material remnants become places from which to piece a history back together.
